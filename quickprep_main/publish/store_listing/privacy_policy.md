## 1. Introduction
QuickPrep ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the QuickPrep mobile application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
QuickPrep operates with an offline-first architecture. The following data is stored locally on your device using `SharedPreferences`:
- Study progress, flashcard Leitner box numbers, and review schedules.
- Daily streak counters, streak freezes, and XP points.
- Bookmarked questions and test score history.
- Local receipt of Pro license entitlement.

### B. Feedback and Typo Submissions
When you use the "Report Typo/Issue" feature, the following information is transmitted:
- Question ID and issue category (e.g. Typo, Clarification).
- Optional user comments describing the suggestion.
- Timestamp of the submission.
No personally identifiable contact details are mandated or transmitted without your explicit action.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

QuickPrep integrates trusted industry SDKs to provide monetization and app stability:

### A. Google AdMob
- We use Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.
- AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or IDFA) to deliver relevant ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.
- Users who upgrade to **QuickPrep Pro** enjoy complete and permanent suppression of all Google AdMob ad requests.

### B. Razorpay Payment Gateway
- Payments for the QuickPrep Pro Lifetime License are processed securely via the Razorpay payment gateway.
- Payment credentials (credit/debit cards, UPI VPA, net banking) are handled directly by Razorpay on PCI-DSS certified servers. QuickPrep does not store, access, or process your raw payment details.

---

## 4. Permissions Used
- **INTERNET**: Required to sync study questions, submit typo reports, serve ads (for free tier), and initiate Razorpay checkout.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Used to detect offline vs online connectivity to prevent failed network calls.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
QuickPrep is targeted towards software developers, college students, and technical interview candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
